One thing more for this round, from the coordinator. It takes precedence over the other two texts where they differ.

In this round, let most of your requests be things done, not things looked up or letters. Where the situation shows something waiting on this seat's own decision or act, do it through `do`, each as its own request: an application waiting to be decided (decide it: grant it or refuse it, naming the application, who applied and what for, and the records your decision rests on); a bill, an invoice, wages or a debt that this seat may pay (pay it, naming the account it comes from, who is paid, the amount, what it settles and the records it rests on); a permit, a licence or a permission this seat may give or take back; a notice, a decision or a ruling this seat is to publish. At most two of your requests for a situation may be to be shown or to read something. Only what the situation gives real ground for: where nothing waits on this seat, write fewer requests, and none is a fine answer.
